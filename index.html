About

Background

I'm Prince Chinyama, a Data Analyst finishing my Master's in Business Analytics at the University of New Hampshire's Peter T. Paul College of Business and Economics. Before graduate school, I spent several years building reporting and analytics functions from the ground up — first as a research assistant preparing survey data for faculty publications, then as a data analyst supporting operations, quality, and logistics teams for clients across South Africa and, more recently, the United States.

My work sits at the intersection of clean data and clear decisions: reconciling messy source systems into analysis-ready datasets, building the Power BI and SQL reporting that executives actually check, and translating technical findings into recommendations that nontechnical stakeholders can act on. I hold a Bachelor's in Information Systems and Mathematical Statistics from Rhodes University in South Africa, and grew up in Zimbabwe.

Currently based in Seattle, WA — open to relocation anywhere in the US.
